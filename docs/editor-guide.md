# K Buzz Newsroom — Editor Guide

The newsroom lives at **https://kbuzz.sanity.studio** (or go to `/admin` on the website).
Sign in with the Google or email account you were invited with.

## Publish a story

1. **All articles → +** (top of the list) to start a new article.
2. Fill in the **Story** tab:
   - **Headline** — up to 120 characters. Click **Generate** next to **Slug** to make the web address.
   - **Subheadline / excerpt** — one or two sentences (max 200). This shows on story cards and in WhatsApp previews.
   - **Category** and **Author** — pick from the list. New authors and categories are added under **Authors** / **Categories** in the left menu.
   - **Published at** — defaults to now. A future time schedules the story: it stays hidden until then and appears on the next site update after that time.
   - **Cover image** — drag a photo in, then fill in **Alt text** (required: describe the photo in a sentence). Use **Edit → hotspot** to mark the important part so crops on phones keep it in frame.
   - **Body** — write the story. The toolbar adds headings, quotes, links, inline images and embeds.
3. Optional, on the **Media & links** tab:
   - **Gallery** — drop several photos at once; each needs alt text. Readers can swipe and open them full screen.
   - **Source URL** — link to the original report; shown as "Originally reported by …".
   - **Embed URL** — a YouTube, SoundCloud or Zeno link shows a player under the cover. X and TikTok links show as a link card.
4. Press **Publish** (bottom right). The website updates itself in about 2 minutes.

## Feature a story on the homepage

Turn on **Featured** in the Story tab. The newest featured story takes the large homepage slot; the next two newest stories stack beside it. If nothing is featured, the newest story takes the slot.

## Edit or unpublish

- Open the article, change it, press **Publish** again. Add an **Updated at** time if the change matters to readers.
- To take a story down: **⋯ menu → Unpublish**. It disappears from the site on the next update (about 2 minutes).

## Photo tips

- Landscape photos at least **1600 px wide** look best as covers. The site resizes them for phones automatically.
- Always credit the photographer in **Credit**.
- Don't upload anything private: published content is public.
